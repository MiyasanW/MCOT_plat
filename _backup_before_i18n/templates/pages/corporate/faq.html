Common Questions

Find answers regarding booking, payments, and equipment policies.

How do I rent equipment?
You can browse our catalog online, check availability for your desired dates, and add items to your cart. Once you submit your booking request, our team will review and confirm it within 24 hours.
What documents are required?
For corporate clients, we require a company registration certificate and VAT registration (Por Por 20). For individuals, a valid Thai National ID card is required.
Can I rent a studio for a half-day?
Yes, our studios are available for both half-day (4 hours) and full-day (8 hours) bookings. Please contact our team for specific pricing.
Do you offer delivery service?
Yes, we offer delivery and pickup services within the Bangkok Metropolitan Region. Additional charges may apply based on distance and equipment volume.
